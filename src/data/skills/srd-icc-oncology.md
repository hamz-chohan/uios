## 1. Background {#background}
**Expectation:** Summarize the clinical context of the unsolicited medical inquiry using the protocol synopsis. State the indication and line of therapy. If the specific inquiry context is not present in the sources, defer to the author rather than inferring it.
**Sources:** protocol
**Mandatory text:** "This response is provided in answer to an unsolicited medical inquiry and is not intended to promote the product."

## 2. Clinical Question {#clinical-question}
**Expectation:** Restate the healthcare professional's question in neutral, precise language. Do not editorialize or expand scope beyond what was asked.
**Sources:** inquiry

## 3. Evidence Summary {#evidence-summary}
**Expectation:** Summarize the pivotal trial evidence relevant to the question, drawing on the clinical study report and investigator brochure. Every efficacy or safety claim must carry a citation to its source. Present balanced efficacy and safety.
**Sources:** csr, ib

## 4. Efficacy Data Table {#efficacy-table}
**Expectation:** Produce a table of the primary and key secondary endpoints (endpoint, arm, result, 95% CI, p-value) from the clinical study report. Preserve reported numbers exactly; do not round or infer missing cells.
**Sources:** csr

## 5. Safety Profile {#safety-profile}
**Expectation:** Summarize the adverse-event profile relevant to the inquiry, including the most common events and any of special interest. Cite the source for each figure.
**Sources:** csr, ib

## 6. Dosing and Administration {#dosing}
**Expectation:** State the approved dosing and administration relevant to the question, sourced from the prescribing information. If prescribing information is not among the sources, defer to the author.
**Sources:** pi
