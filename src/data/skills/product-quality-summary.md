## 1. Product Identification {#identification}
**Expectation:** State the application number, applicant, proprietary and established names, dosage form and strength, and route of administration, exactly as they appear in the review. Do not infer any value that is not stated.
**Sources:** quality-review

## 2. Regulatory History {#regulatory-history}
**Expectation:** Summarize the submission history: original submission, any Complete Response action and its stated deficiency, the resubmission, and the current regulatory action. Cite the dates as reported.
**Sources:** quality-review

## 3. Container Closure and Leachables {#leachables}
**Expectation:** Summarize the leachables assessment for the proposed container closure system: what the Complete Response required, what the applicant submitted, the storage conditions and durations under which batches were assessed, and the review team's conclusion on adequacy. Preserve all storage conditions verbatim, including temperature, relative humidity, and orientation.
**Sources:** quality-review

## 4. Stability and Retest Period {#stability}
**Expectation:** Summarize the stability data supporting the drug product and any change to the drug substance retest period. Report study durations and conditions exactly as stated. If the review does not state a value, defer to the author rather than estimating it.
**Sources:** quality-review

## 5. Quality Conclusion {#quality-conclusion}
**Expectation:** State the Office of Pharmaceutical Quality recommendation and the basis given for it, including whether manufacturing and testing facilities were found acceptable. Quote the recommendation term used in the review.
**Sources:** quality-review

## 6. Labeling Statement Requirements {#labeling-requirements}
**Expectation:** State the labeling content requirements that apply to this product class, drawn from the applicable FDA guidance. Identify which sections must use the product's proprietary name and which must use the proper name. Include the required biosimilarity footnote verbatim if the product is a biosimilar; if the product is not a biosimilar, say so explicitly and do not include the footnote.
**Sources:** biosimilar-guidance
**Mandatory text:** "This summary reflects the FDA review record and is not a substitute for the approved labeling."

## 7. Storage and Handling {#storage-handling}
**Expectation:** State the approved storage and handling conditions for the marketed product as they appear in the prescribing information.
**Sources:** pi
