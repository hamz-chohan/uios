## Inquiry text (p.1 §Verbatim)

"I am writing regarding one of my patients, a 58-year-old woman with locally advanced
intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma whose tumor carries an FGFR2-BICC1 fusion. She has
progressed after first-line gemcitabine and cisplatin and, more recently, after a
second-line fluoropyrimidine regimen. Her performance status remains good (ECOG 1)
and she is keen to consider further systemic options.

I understand Oncorafenib is a selective FGFR2 inhibitor being studied in this
setting. **Could you please share what efficacy has been reported for Oncorafenib in
previously treated FGFR2-fusion-positive intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma -
specifically the objective response rate and the durability of response - and what
the principal safety considerations are that I should discuss with my patient?**

I would also appreciate any information on how progression-free and overall survival
have read out in the pivotal study, if those data are available. I am aware the
product may not yet be approved in my region; this request is for my own clinical
decision-making and is not a request for promotional material."
