# Clinical Protocol Synopsis - Study ONC-401-202

**Sponsor document - confidential.** Protocol ONC-401-202, Version 4.0.
Investigational product: **Oncorafenib (ONC-401)**, an oral selective FGFR2-fusion
kinase inhibitor.

## 1. Title (p.1 §1)

A Phase 2, open-label, single-arm, multicenter study of Oncorafenib (ONC-401) in
adult patients with locally advanced or metastatic intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma
(ICC) harboring fibroblast growth factor receptor 2 (FGFR2) fusions or
rearrangements who have progressed on prior systemic therapy.

## 2. Background and Rationale (p.1 §2)

Intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma is a rare, aggressive biliary tract cancer with
limited options after failure of first-line gemcitabine-based chemotherapy. FGFR2
fusions occur in approximately 10-16% of ICC and are oncogenic drivers. Oncorafenib
is an ATP-competitive small-molecule inhibitor with highest potency against FGFR2
and is hypothesized to produce durable objective responses in this molecularly
defined population.

## 3. Objectives and Endpoints (p.2 §3)

**Primary objective:** to estimate the anti-tumor activity of Oncorafenib as measured
by objective response rate (ORR).

- **Primary endpoint:** ORR by independent central review (ICR) per RECIST v1.1,
  defined as the proportion of patients with a confirmed complete or partial response.
  The pre-specified null hypothesis is ORR ≤ 15%.

**Secondary objectives:** to characterize durability, survival, and safety.

- **Key secondary endpoints:** duration of response (DoR), disease control rate
  (DCR), progression-free survival (PFS) by ICR, and overall survival (OS).
- **Safety endpoints:** incidence and severity of treatment-emergent adverse events
  (TEAEs) graded per NCI-CTCAE v5.0, including adverse events of special interest.

## 4. Study Population (p.2 §4)

**Planned enrollment:** approximately 108 patients.

**Key inclusion criteria (p.3 §4.1):**

1. Age ≥ 18 years.
2. Histologically confirmed locally advanced or metastatic ICC not amenable to
   curative surgery or radiation.
3. Documented FGFR2 fusion or rearrangement by a validated assay.
4. Radiographic progression on at least one prior line of systemic therapy,
   including a gemcitabine-containing regimen.
5. Measurable disease per RECIST v1.1.
6. ECOG performance status 0-1.
7. Adequate hepatic, renal, and hematologic function.

**Key exclusion criteria (p.3 §4.2):**

1. Prior treatment with a selective FGFR inhibitor.
2. Current corneal or retinal disorder, including serous retinal detachment.
3. Serum phosphate above the institutional upper limit of normal at screening that
   cannot be corrected.

## 5. Treatment (p.4 §5.1)

Oncorafenib is administered orally at **20 mg once daily** in continuous 21-day
cycles until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or withdrawal. Dose
interruption and two dose-reduction levels (16 mg, 12 mg) are permitted for
management of toxicity, including protocol-specified management of hyperphosphatemia.

## 6. Statistical Considerations (p.4 §6)

A sample of approximately 103 response-evaluable patients provides greater than 90%
power to reject the null ORR of 15% at a one-sided alpha of 0.025 if the true ORR is
30% or higher. Efficacy analyses are performed on the response-evaluable population;
safety analyses on all patients who receive at least one dose.
